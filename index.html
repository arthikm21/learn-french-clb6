Free CLB 6 French course — learn French free for TCF / TEF Canada

Bonjour! is a free, no-signup French course built for one outcome: reaching CLB 6 (roughly B1) across all four skills on the TCF Canada and TEF Canada exams. It is made for Express Entry candidates, federal job seekers, and anyone who needs Canadian Language Benchmark 6 in French — with Canadian French neural audio, structured four-skill practice, and no tracking.

What you can practise

Phonics and minimal-pair ear training, themed vocabulary with spaced repetition, grammar from A1 to B1, graded listening and reading, speaking shadowing with record-and-playback self-review, writing structure checks, and practice based on TCF Canada speaking and writing task formats — all ordered into an eight-phase path with gate quizzes between phases.

Common questions

Is the course free? Yes. The course is free, with no signup, email, or payment required.

How long does NCLC 6 take? Timing depends on your starting point, practice quality, and access to human feedback. Beginners should expect many months rather than a shortcut.

Does it use Canadian French? Yes. Listening models use Canadian French neural voices, with browser speech synthesis only as a fallback.
